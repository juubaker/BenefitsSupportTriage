# Medical Plan Eligibility

## Who is eligible

Full-time employees working 30 or more hours per week are eligible for medical,
dental, and vision coverage on the first of the month following their hire date.
Part-time employees working fewer than 20 hours per week are not eligible for
medical coverage but may be eligible for voluntary benefits.

## Adding dependents

Eligible dependents include a legal spouse, a domestic partner, and children up
to age 26. Children may remain on the plan until the end of the month in which
they turn 26. Documentation (marriage certificate, birth certificate, or proof
of domestic partnership) is required within 31 days of the qualifying event.

# Open Enrollment

## Enrollment window

Open enrollment runs for two weeks each fall. Elections made during open
enrollment take effect on January 1 of the following year. Outside of open
enrollment, changes are only permitted within 31 days of a qualifying life
event.

## Qualifying life events

Qualifying life events include marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child,
loss of other coverage, and a change in employment status. An employee has 31
days from the date of the event to make a corresponding change to their
elections.

# Health Savings Account (HSA)

## Eligibility and limits

An HSA is available only to employees enrolled in a qualifying high-deductible
health plan (HDHP). Employees enrolled in any other medical plan, or who are
enrolled in Medicare, are not eligible to contribute to an HSA. Annual
contribution limits are set by the IRS and may change each year.
